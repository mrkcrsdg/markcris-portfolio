Project Proposal

Prepared by: Mark Cris Ubana

Clients: Students, Friends & Family

Introduction

I'm excited to help you design a matching flyer and poster set that truly feels like Tambayan Café. My goal is to capture the cozy, welcoming atmosphere where students come to study, and friends and families gather to catch up. Through clean visuals and friendly, clear messaging, we'll make sure your best menu offers stand out, instantly grab attention, and give people a great reason to drop by, order their favorite treats, or hang out.

Deliverables

Flyer – The first deliverable is a handheld menu flyer featuring an earthy coffee-brown background, minimalist coffee cup illustration, and floating coffee bean graphics. Designed for quick reference, it clearly highlights the café name (Tambayan Café), daily store hours (9 AM to 10 PM), and an itemized price list for core espresso drinks—Espresso (PHP 110), Americano (PHP 120), Cappuccino (PHP 150), Latte (PHP 130), and Mocha (PHP 140)—making it easy for students to check budget-friendly options.

Poster – The second deliverable is an eye-catching display poster built on a clean white background with bold, retro-inspired typography reading "coffee shop" and a central brown emblem for Tambayan Café. It uses prominent coffee bean illustrations, a badge highlighting "OPEN DAILY", and the schedule (09:00 AM - 10:00 PM) to serve as an inviting, high-contrast window advertisement that catches the attention of students and passersby.

Page 01 of 02

Why Print Media Matters for Tambayan Café

  • • It catches students and locals right where they are: Handing out flyers or putting up window posters immediately grabs the attention of people already walking past campus or around the neighborhood who are looking for a quick caffeine fix or a place to study.
  • • It builds everyday brand recognition: When students see the same cool poster on their daily walk to class or keep a menu flyer on their desk, Tambayan Café naturally becomes the go-to spot whenever someone asks, "Where should we hang out?"
  • • It doesn't rely on social media algorithms: Unlike an Instagram post that gets buried in a feed after a few hours, a physical poster on a bulletin board or a flyer pinned in a dorm stays visible for weeks without needing likes or shares to be seen.
  • • It gives a clear call to action: A simple, well-made print piece that gives people a direct reason to drop by—whether that's checking out the affordable price list, trying a daily brew, or coming in during opening hours to finish a group project.
